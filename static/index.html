BLUEPRINT

Reverse-engineer any app from public signals into a build-and-beat playbook.

Public signals only. BLUEPRINT infers architecture from observable evidence (fingerprints, headers, DNS/TLS, public GitHub, pricing). It honors robots.txt and does not touch the dark web, decompile binaries, bypass DRM, or scrape behind logins. “Deep recon” adds opt-in GraphQL/endpoint probing.
